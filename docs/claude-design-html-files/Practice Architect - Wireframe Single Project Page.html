03 / Project Overview

House on the Cliff
Cornwall

Private Residence · 480 m² · Coastal · United Kingdom

Status Completed
Year 2024
Location Cornwall, UK
Typology Private Residence
Area 480 m²
Client Private
Team P. Archer (Lead)
R. Okafor, M. Lin
Awards Civic Trust · 2024
AJ100 Shortlist

A timber and concrete residence perched on a granite headland — a building that reads the weather as much as the site.

The brief asked for a long, low house that would sit lightly against the cliff edge and frame the Atlantic without competing with it. The plan is a single linear bar, splayed along the contour, with bedroom wings folded back into the slope and living spaces cantilevered out toward the sea.

Inside, board-formed concrete walls hold their cool gray light against the warmth of cedar joinery and brushed oak floors. A continuous clerestory traces the spine of the building, washing the inner wall and registering the passage of cloud across the day.

External material was kept to a tight palette — local granite at the plinth, pre-weathered larch at the cladding, oxidised steel at openings — a deliberate restraint that lets the landscape do most of the work.
